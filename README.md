## About me

I'm a Data Engineer based in Germany with 10+ years of experience building data systems. My journey spans the evolution of the field—from Informatica PowerCenter and Hadoop/Spark to cloud-native platforms on GCP and AWS, and now Agentic AI.

At **FINN**, I help build and evolve the core data platform on GCP, combining modern data engineering with AI-assisted workflows and autonomous problem resolution. Previously, I worked at Uplight, Clairvoyant (embedded with PayPal), and TCS.

Outside work, I share practical lessons about data engineering, architecture trade-offs, AI-assisted engineering, career growth, and life in Germany through **Rishi Bytes**.

## What I focus on

- **Reliable data platforms:** scalable batch and streaming pipelines, observability, testing, and trusted data products
- **Cloud architecture:** GCP and AWS systems designed for operability, cost awareness, and long-term maintainability
- **Agentic AI:** production workflows where agents assist investigation, automation, and autonomous problem resolution
- **Knowledge sharing:** honest engineering lessons through articles, videos, and open-source experiments
